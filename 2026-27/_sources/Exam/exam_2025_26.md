# Exam for Students Enrolled in A.Y. 2025-2026

This page is dedicated to the students who **enrolled in the A.Y. 2025-2026** and who still have to take the exam of Statistical and Mathematical Methods for Machine Learning. For these students, a **transitional exam modality**, similar to the one of the last year, is available.

## Modality

- The exam consists of a **quiz only**, taken **in the classroom**, on the exam dates published on **AlmaEsami**.
- The quiz contains questions related both to the topics of the **quiz** and to the topics of the **oral exam** of the last year.
- **No homework** is required.
- The quiz is the **complete exam** of the course: it covers the whole program of the A.Y. 2025-2026, therefore it is **not** necessary to take also the exam of Module 1.

```{important}
This modality is available **only during the first semester**, i.e. in the **two exam sessions of the winter session**. After that, the students enrolled in the A.Y. 2025-2026 will have to take the exam with the same modality as all the other students (see the [Homework](homework) page).
```

## Program

The program of the exam is the one of the A.Y. 2025-2026, and the corresponding teaching materials are available at [https://devangelista2.github.io/statistical-mathematical-methods/2025-26/](https://devangelista2.github.io/statistical-mathematical-methods/2025-26/).
